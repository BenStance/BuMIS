BuMIS Buying and Selling Workflow Writeup
Date: August 12, 2026

Purpose

This document explains how the current project operates today for buying, selling, stock in, and stock out.
It also describes the changes needed for phase two based on the requested new workflow:

1. Buying should create a purchase invoice, but it should not be marked as paid automatically.
2. Payment should be a separate action and must be recorded in the ledger.
3. There should be a settings option for auto payment after invoice creation.
4. Selling should follow the same pattern:
   - invoice first,
   - payment separately,
   - optional auto payment based on settings.
5. The system should add a Purchase Invoice and Payment Voucher.
6. The selling invoice already exists, but Receipt generation must be added.

1. Current System Overview

BuMIS is currently built as a sales-focused business management system.
The existing codebase centers on:

- Sales invoice creation
- Inventory stock updates
- Ledger posting for sales invoices
- Product, customer, vendor, and inventory management
- Business settings and invoice formatting

The current design supports multi-tenant business data through a BusinessId field across most operational tables.
The current implementation is strongest on selling, stock reduction, and bookkeeping for sales transactions.

2. What Exists Today For Selling

The current sales flow is implemented in the backend invoice module and supported by the frontend invoice workspace.

2.1 Sales Invoice Creation

The current invoice creation flow is in the backend invoices service.
When a sales invoice is created:

- A unique invoice number is generated using settings such as prefix, year inclusion, and padding.
- The invoice is saved with header values such as:
  - customer
  - invoice date
  - due date
  - payment method
  - subtotal
  - discount total
  - tax total
  - total amount
  - amount paid
  - balance
  - status
- Invoice items are saved separately.
- The invoice can be created as a draft or as a posted transaction.

Important current behavior:

- If `isDraft = true`, the invoice is saved as a draft and does not affect stock or ledger.
- If `isDraft = false`, the system posts the invoice immediately and performs stock and ledger updates.

2.2 Payment Behavior For Sales

The current payment logic is mixed into invoice creation.

- Payment methods currently available are `cash` and `credit`.
- If the invoice is created with `cash`, the invoice is immediately marked as `paid`.
- If the invoice is created with `credit`, the invoice is marked as `posted`.
- For cash invoices, `amountPaid` is set to the full invoice total automatically.
- For credit invoices, `amountPaid` is zero and `balance` equals the full total.

This means the system does not currently have a separate payment transaction for sales.
Payment is not recorded as an independent operation in the ledger.

2.3 Stock Out On Sales

When a sales invoice is not a draft, the system reduces inventory automatically.

For each item on the invoice:

- The product current stock is checked before the invoice is saved.
- If stock is insufficient, invoice creation fails.
- If stock is available, product stock is reduced by the sold quantity.
- An inventory transaction is recorded with type `stock_out`.
- The inventory transaction stores:
  - previous stock
  - new stock
  - quantity
  - invoice reference
  - product reference
  - user who created it

This means stock out is currently tied directly to sales invoice posting.

2.4 Ledger Posting On Sales

When the invoice is posted, the system creates ledger entries automatically.

Current ledger behavior for sales:

- If payment method is cash, the debit is posted to Cash.
- If payment method is credit, the debit is posted to Accounts Receivable.
- Sales Revenue is credited.
- Discounts are posted to the Discounts expense account.
- Tax is posted to Tax Payable when tax exists.

This is good sales accounting for the current model, but payment itself is not yet separated from invoice creation.
The invoice creation event is doing both:

- sales posting
- stock movement
- accounting posting

2.5 Invoice Cancellation

If a non-draft invoice is cancelled:

- stock is restored
- reversal ledger entries are created
- the invoice status becomes cancelled
- a cancellation audit log is recorded

Draft invoices can be cancelled without reversing stock or ledger, because they have not been posted yet.

2.6 Invoice Printing

The frontend already supports invoice print output.
The invoice details page and print preview show:

- invoice number
- customer
- items
- totals
- amount paid
- balance
- payment method

So the current sales invoice can already be printed as a customer-facing invoice document.

3. What Exists Today For Buying / Stock In

This is the most important gap in the current system.

3.1 Buying Is Not A Real Purchase Module Yet

There is no dedicated purchase invoice module in the backend.
There is no purchase invoice entity, purchase invoice items entity, purchase payment entity, or payment voucher entity.

The current system only has:

- vendors as master data
- inventory stock in as a manual stock movement
- vendor profile pages that show purchase history placeholders

So at the moment, buying is not a true financial workflow.
It is only a manual stock-in operation.

3.2 Manual Stock In

The inventory module has a stock-in action.

Current stock-in behavior:

- A product is selected
- Quantity is entered
- Unit cost is optional
- Vendor is optional
- Reference number is optional
- Stock in date is optional
- Remarks are optional
- Product current stock is increased
- An inventory transaction of type `stock_in` is created

Important:

- Stock in currently updates inventory only.
- It does not create a purchase invoice.
- It does not create a payment record.
- It does not create a voucher.
- It does not create ledger entries.

So buying is not yet connected to accounting in the same way that sales is connected.

3.3 Vendor Module Today

The vendor module is only a supplier master-data module.

Current vendor behavior:

- create vendor
- update vendor
- archive vendor
- view vendor profile

The vendor profile includes `purchaseHistory`, but it is currently an empty placeholder.
That confirms there is no actual vendor purchase transaction history being stored yet.

4. Current Settings Behavior

System settings already exist, but they do not yet support automatic payment rules.

Current invoice-related settings include:

- invoice prefix

- include year in invoice number
- walk-in customer name
- print layout
- footer notes
- payment instructions

Current tax settings include:

- tax enabled

Current currency settings include:

What is missing in settings for phase two:

- auto payment toggle for purchase invoices

- payment mode defaults

6. Recommended Phase Two Workflow

This section describes the target operation you asked for.

6.1 Buying Flow

The buying process should be split into two separate actions:

Step 1: Purchase Invoice

- User creates a purchase invoice
- Invoice is saved in the system
- Invoice is recorded in the ledger
- Invoice is not automatically marked as paid
- Stock in may happen here or on posting, depending on the chosen business rule

Step 2: Payment Voucher

- User records payment separately
- Payment is linked to the purchase invoice
- Payment is recorded in the ledger
- The purchase balance is reduced
- The invoice status updates based on payment amount

Optional auto payment:

- If settings say auto payment is enabled, then once the purchase invoice is created, the payment voucher is created automatically too
- If settings say auto payment is disabled, the invoice stays unpaid until the payment voucher is posted manually

6.2 Selling Flow

Selling should also be split into two separate actions:

Step 1: Sales Invoice

- User creates sales invoice
- Invoice is recorded
- Ledger entries for the invoice are created
- Stock out is handled
- The invoice is not necessarily paid yet

Step 2: Receipt / Payment

- User records payment separately
- Receipt is generated
- Payment is recorded in the ledger
- Customer balance is reduced
- Invoice status becomes partially paid or paid

Optional auto payment:

- If settings say auto payment is enabled, the system automatically records payment after invoice creation
- If settings say auto payment is disabled, payment must be posted manually

6.3 Receipt Generation

For the selling side, the missing document is the receipt.

The invoice already exists, so the new work should add:

- receipt entity or receipt output logic
- receipt number
- receipt print layout
- receipt PDF generation
- link between receipt and sales invoice
- receipt ledger posting

7. Suggested Data Objects For Phase Two

To support the new workflow, the system will likely need new database objects.

7.1 Buying Side

Recommended new objects:

- PurchaseInvoice
- PurchaseInvoiceItem
- PaymentVoucher
- PurchaseLedgerEntry links
- Purchase stock-in reference

7.2 Selling Side

Recommended new objects:

- Receipt
- ReceiptItem if needed
- Sales payment record
- Receipt ledger entry links

7.3 Settings

Recommended new settings:

- `purchase.auto_payment_enabled`
- `sales.auto_payment_enabled`
- `purchase.invoice_prefix`
- `receipt.number_prefix`
- `voucher.number_prefix`
- `payment.default_method`

8. Current Behavior Summary In Plain Words

Right now, the project works like this:

- Selling is already implemented as a live sales invoice system.
- When a sales invoice is posted, the system reduces stock and creates ledger entries automatically.
- Cash sales are marked paid immediately.
- Credit sales are posted with an unpaid balance.
- There is no separate payment step for sales yet.
- Buying is not yet a full purchase workflow.
- Stock in exists only as a manual inventory movement.
- Vendors exist as master data only.
- There is no purchase invoice.
- There is no payment voucher.
- There is no receipt generation for sales.

9. Phase Two Implementation Goal

The phase-two goal should be to move from a single-step transaction model to a two-step accounting model:

For buying:

1. Create purchase invoice
2. Record payment separately through payment voucher
3. Optionally auto-post payment from settings

For selling:

1. Create sales invoice
2. Record payment separately through receipt
3. Optionally auto-post payment from settings

This will make the system more correct for accounting, easier to audit, and closer to real-world business operations.

10. Final Note

The current codebase is already a solid base for this upgrade because:

- inventory transactions already exist
- ledger entries already exist
- invoice numbering and settings already exist
- vendors and customers already exist

So phase two does not need a full rewrite.
It needs new purchase and payment workflows, plus a better separation between invoice creation and payment posting.
